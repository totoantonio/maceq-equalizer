No driver. No system changes.

Most Mac equalizers make you install a virtual audio driver and reroute your output device. MacEQ doesn't. It uses Apple's native CATapDescription audio tap API — the same mechanism macOS uses for screen recording audio.

◎

Every app, simultaneously

One EQ for the whole system. Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, any browser, any app — all processed at once, no per-app setup.

✓

Nothing installed, nothing broken

No kernel extension, no virtual device in your Sound settings. Toggle it off and your original audio routing returns in real time.

◌

Bluetooth & AirPods

Full support for AirPods and Bluetooth speakers, not just wired output. A confirmation tone plays on activation so you know it's live.

▾

Lives in your menu bar

Always one click away. Adjust the curve, switch presets, or set system volume without leaving what you're doing.

A real 10-band parametric EQ

32 Hz to 16 kHz, ±12 dB, with the readouts you need to actually know what you're doing.

〜

Live EQ frequency curve

A smooth bezier line drawing your exact EQ shape, redrawn as you drag. Blue fill for boost, purple for cut, with dB grid lines at +12, +6, 0, −6 and −12.

▮

Live spectrum analyzer

A green overlay showing real-time audio energy per band — drawn separately from your EQ curve, so you can see what the music is doing and what you're doing to it.

+

Additive quick actions

  • Clarity — lifts presence and air, also fully reversible
  • Reset EQ — back to flat

Save the whole session, not just the curve

Hit Save Settings and MacEQ stores your entire configuration under one name — EQ bands, spatial surround, stereo width, crossfeed, channel balance, effects, pitch, volume boost and auto level, together. Your presets appear under MY PRESETS at the top of the Presets tab. One tap restores every setting at once.

Studio processing, not gimmicks

The stereo tools here are the ones mastering engineers actually use.

◑

Stereo Width (M/S)

True Mid-Side processing from 0% mono to 200% super-wide — the same technique used in professional mastering studios, not a fake widener.

◐

Crossfeed

Blends 30% of each channel into the opposite ear, removing the "sound is inside my skull" effect that makes long headphone sessions tiring. A standard audiophile feature.

⬡

Spatial Surround

Virtual 5-channel surround with an animated speaker diagram — FL, FC, FR, RL, RR — showing sonar pulse waves as the field moves.

◈

Dolby Stereo

Plate reverb processing for cinematic stereo expansion. Pairs well with the Movie preset.

↕

Auto Level

Software AGC holds every track at consistent loudness. No more diving for the volume key when a loud ad or a badly mastered song hits.

↑

Volume Boost

Push output to 100–200% beyond the macOS system maximum via the mixer node — for quiet laptop speakers, quiet recordings, and quiet meetings.

☾

Night Mode & Ambience

Night Mode rolls off harsh highs for late listening. Ambience adds natural small-room depth and presence.

✧

Fidelity & Pitch Shift

Fidelity is a high-shelf boost that restores air and micro-detail. Pitch Shift moves pitch ±12 semitones without touching playback speed.

⇄

Channel Balance

Independent left/right level control — for hearing compensation, or for headphones that simply aren't matched anymore.
